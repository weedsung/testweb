## GoogleTrendArchive pilot B

- Fixed window: 2024-11-28 to 2026-01-03
- Countries: KR, JP, TW, SG, US, GB
- Rows: 520,933
- Unique trends: 173,913
- Complete episode median duration: JP 1.50h, GB 1.83h, US 2.17h, KR 2.67h, TW 2.83h, SG 3.83h.
- Korea was not the shortest in any of the 15 observed months.
- The result weakens a broad Korea-fastest claim, but does not replace the preregistered V/H test.
