- Domains: technology, investment, food, entertainment, politics, sports.
- Languages: Korean, Japanese, Traditional/Simplified Chinese, English.
- Main classifier: fixed lexicon over trend text plus trend breakdown.
- Sensitivity classifier: trend text only.
- Ambiguous multi-hit rows are excluded from domain estimates.
- Minimum complete episodes: 300 per country-domain, 100 per country-domain-month.
- Main metric: complete non-estimated episode duration between 0 and 48 hours.
